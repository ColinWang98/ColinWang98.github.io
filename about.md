## Biography

I am a passionate HCI researcher focused on **virtual environments** and **urban culture restoration**. Currently working as a Research Assistant with **Prof. Li Chen Richard** at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, where I completed my Master's degree in Sustainable Urban Development (SUD).

My research lies at the intersection of **Human-Computer Interaction**, **Extended Reality**, and **Cultural Heritage Preservation**. I am particularly interested in how immersive technologies can create meaningful connections between people and their cultural heritage, and how virtual environments can serve therapeutic and educational purposes.

### Research Philosophy
*"Technology should serve as a bridge between tradition and innovation, creating immersive experiences that preserve our cultural heritage while advancing human understanding and wellbeing."*
