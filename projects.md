## Development Projects

### Digital Heritage & Cultural Preservation

<div class="project-item">
  <h4>🏛️ Yigeng Murals Digital Activation</h4>
  <div class="project-tags">
    <span class="badge">Mobile App</span>
    <span class="badge">Cultural Heritage</span>
    <span class="badge">Digital Art</span>
  </div>
  
  <p><strong>Duration:</strong> 09/2019 - 06/2020 | <strong>Role:</strong> Idea generation, task coordination, technical lead</p>
  
  <p>Developed a mobile application to promote local culture and protect Taihang Murals through digital activation and integration into modern life. The app provides virtual tours, historical context, and interactive features to engage younger audiences with traditional art forms.</p>
  
  <p><strong>Achievement:</strong> 🏆 3rd Prize, "Challenge Cup" Hebei Province College Student Entrepreneurship Contest</p>
  
  <p><strong>Impact:</strong> Successfully increased local cultural awareness and tourist engagement with heritage sites.</p>
</div>

<div class="project-item">
  <h4>🏘️ Yuanqu Village Rural Revitalization</h4>
  <div class="project-tags">
    <span class="badge">Urban Planning</span>
    <span class="badge">Community Design</span>
    <span class="badge">Heritage Conservation</span>
  </div>
  
  <p><strong>Duration:</strong> 09/2020 - 06/2021 | <strong>Role:</strong> Research coordination, progress management, report writing</p>
  
  <p>Participated in the comprehensive revitalization of Yuanqu Village in Henan Province through a design-for-equity model. The project achieved remarkable success in heritage conservation, tourism revival, and sustainable commercial operations.</p>
  
  <p><strong>Recognition:</strong> 🎯 Approved by 2020 Hebei Province College Student Innovation & Entrepreneur Training Project</p>
  
  <p><strong>Outcomes:</strong> Successful integration of traditional architecture preservation with modern sustainable development practices.</p>
</div>
