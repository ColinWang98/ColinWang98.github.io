    <h3>The Red MetaChamber (元界·红楼)</h3>
    <div class="project-tags">
      <span class="badge">VR/AR</span>
      <span class="badge">Cultural Heritage</span>
      <span class="badge">Metaverse</span>
      <span class="badge">AI Integration</span>
    </div>
    
    <p><strong>Supervisor:</strong> Dr. Richard Li Chen</p>
    <p><strong>Platform:</strong> Spatial.io | <strong>Duration:</strong> 2024</p>
    
    <p>A groundbreaking metaverse project that brings the classic Chinese literature "Dream of the Red Chamber" to life through virtual reality. The project presents the iconic Xiao Xiang Guan (潇湘馆) scene with immersive interactive elements, AI-powered character dialogues, and traditional Chinese music appreciation.</p>

    <h4>🎯 Project Objectives</h4>
    <ul>
      <li>Preserve and digitalize traditional Chinese architectural culture</li>
      <li>Create immersive cultural heritage experiences</li>
      <li>Demonstrate the potential of metaverse in cultural education</li>
      <li>Bridge traditional literature with modern technology</li>
    </ul>

    <h4>🛠️ Technical Implementation</h4>
    <p><strong>Development Stack:</strong> Unity, SketchUp, Blender, Spatial.io</p>
    <p><strong>AI Technologies:</strong> Jimeng AI (character dialogues), Suno AI (music generation)</p>
    <p><strong>Design Reference:</strong> "Move to the Red Chamber" (移步红楼)</p>

    <h4>✨ Key Features</h4>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Scene Interaction:</strong> Users can explore traditional Chinese architecture with interactive educational tags</li>
      <li><strong>Character Dialogues:</strong> AI-generated conversations with main characters (Jia Baoyu, Lin Daiyu, Liu Laolao, Xue Baochai)</li>
      <li><strong>Music Appreciation:</strong> Traditional Chinese music "Ode to the Fallen Blossoms" (葬花吟) generated by Suno AI</li>
      <li><strong>Cultural Learning:</strong> Immersive introduction to Chinese architectural and literary culture</li>
    </ul>

    <h4>🔬 Research Focus</h4>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Bio-responsive Interaction:</strong> Real-time breathing pattern detection and feedback</li>
      <li><strong>Implicit/Explicit Cues:</strong> Multi-modal guidance systems for optimal breathing</li>
      <li><strong>Therapeutic VR:</strong> Nature-based environments for stress reduction and wellness</li>
      <li><strong>User Experience:</strong> Intuitive interaction design for healthcare applications</li>
    </ul>

    <h4>💡 Innovation Highlights</h4>
    <ul>
      <li>Novel framework for respiratory therapy in virtual environments</li>
      <li>Integration of physiological sensors with VR interfaces</li>
      <li>Evidence-based design for therapeutic effectiveness</li>
      <li>Accessible healthcare technology for home use</li>
    </ul>

    <h4>🎯 Expected Outcomes</h4>
    <ul>
      <li>Improved breathing training compliance and effectiveness</li>
      <li>Reduced anxiety and stress through immersive therapy</li>
      <li>Scalable VR healthcare solution</li>
      <li>Publications in HCI and healthcare technology journals</li>
    </ul>

<div class="project-item">
  <h4>🏛️ Yigeng Murals Digital Activation</h4>
  <div class="project-tags">
    <span class="badge">Mobile App</span>
    <span class="badge">Cultural Heritage</span>
    <span class="badge">Digital Art</span>
  </div>
  
  <p><strong>Duration:</strong> 09/2019 - 06/2020 | <strong>Role:</strong> Idea generation, task coordination, technical lead</p>
  
  <p>Developed a mobile application to promote local culture and protect Taihang Murals through digital activation and integration into modern life. The app provides virtual tours, historical context, and interactive features to engage younger audiences with traditional art forms.</p>
  
  <p><strong>Achievement:</strong> 🏆 3rd Prize, "Challenge Cup" Hebei Province College Student Entrepreneurship Contest</p>
  
  <p><strong>Impact:</strong> Successfully increased local cultural awareness and tourist engagement with heritage sites.</p>
</div>
